Skill Health Scores

Progression rank is the skill level (skills) or trained points (stats). Use counts are engagement telemetry only and no longer affect how hard anything is to raise. Chances are shown at a neutral bonus; critical successes roll higher.

Read Expected Rank with care. It inverts the current curve over a lifetime use counter, and it does so under two assumptions that weaken it. It assumes one progression roll per recorded use, so it becomes a lower bound as uncontested firing grows. And it saturates: once a subject's lifetime uses exceed the cost of reaching the soft cap, Expected Rank pins at the soft cap and stays there, so a well-played character below the cap reads as permanently behind whether or not anything is wrong. Counters accumulated before a retune were also earned under a different curve and are not comparable to it. Treat deviation and stall as triage that points at what to look at, never as a measurement of what is broken.
