# U8 action-cost model evidence

**Status:** Owner selected the **Midpoint** package on 2026-08-17. These are the
only U8 bases Task 2 is authorized to encode.

This evidence is generated by
`tools/balance/unified_resolution_model.py`. The script reads shipped scalar
values from `_datafiles/config.yaml` and the tracked anonymized live-character
band from `tools/playtest/profiles/veteran.yaml`; it never reads production
saves. The profile README documents that band as sanitized from a
Meirok-class archive.

The pressure score is the arithmetic mean of gross U8 debit divided by the
maximum pool left unreserved at the shipped reservation ceiling, across all
five fixtures and the special-combat, ranged, rhetoric, sneak, and grapple
traces. The model found 16,800 packages that pass every quantitative gate, then
selected the lowest, highest, and score nearest their arithmetic midpoint with
the specified lexicographic tie-break.

The selected midpoint package puts average modeled U8
pressure near the center of the passing range, leaves the laden novice with 87
Stamina after ten controlled-grapple rounds, and avoids the high package's
edge case where that same novice reaches exactly zero on round ten. It is still
a deliberate upper-bound choice for special moves at four ordinary swings.

The pool formulas are read from live code and use shipped coefficients:

```text
StaminaMax    = 5 + Vitality x 3 + Willpower x 1 + Strength x 0
ConvictionMax = 5 + Charisma x 3 + Willpower x 1
```

The shipped reservation cap is 66%, so the worst legal unreserved maxima are
138, 152, 187, 240, and 172 Stamina and 138, 152, 187, 240, and 178 Conviction
for the rows above. Player Stamina and Conviction regeneration are each 2% of
raw maximum per `AutoHeal` hook, floored to an integer with a minimum of one.
The hook runs every third round. Combat Stamina regeneration is quartered;
Conviction regeneration is not.

The shipped `SpecialMoveCooldown` is 4. `SneakFailCooldown` is absent from
shipped YAML; its `ConfigInt` therefore loads as zero, and live validation
preserves zero because it defaults only negative values. The model consequently
permits one detected sneak attempt every round, never more than one action in a
round. This effective shipped zero is important to the sneak pressure result.

All shipped ranged weapons have one-projectile capacity because live state is a
single `Item.Loaded` boolean. The exact thirty-round ranged sequence is:

## Accepted and rejected boundaries

- **Special:** 0.5, 0.75, and 1.0 fail because the move is not dearer than one
  ordinary swing. Base 4 is the accepted upper boundary at exactly four swings;
  nothing above 4 exists in the approved grid.
- **Shoot and reload:** reload bases that exceed 75% of shoot fail directly.
  Remaining low sums can fail the 5% thirty-round floor for the most efficient
  fixture, while high sums can fail the 35% ceiling for the least efficient
  fixture. Low/Midpoint use 2 + 1; High uses 3.5 + 0.5.
- **Sneak:** the whole 0.5 to 4 physical grid can pass the action-level gates.
  Its pressure contribution is unusually sensitive because the effective
  shipped failure cooldown is zero; this is why the three score-selected rows
  span 0.5, 2.5, and 4.
- **Rhetoric:** bases 1 through 3 fail the 5% floor for the synthetic-high
  fixture at the reservation ceiling. Bases 6 and above fail the 35% ceiling
  for the novice. Bases 4 and 5 are the only passing boundaries.
- **Grapple:** 0.5 through 5 preserve the 2:1 role ratio and reach ten rounds.
  Base 5 is accepted only at the exact depletion boundary described above.
- **Product clamp:** for every package, every physical laden-novice product is
  at or below the shipped `CostTotalMultiplierMax` of 6.0. Base remains outside
  that product clamp, matching live `costs.Calc`.

## Owner rationale

The owner selected **Midpoint** with this rationale:

> The listed values are config bases before the shared `costs.Calc`
> encumbrance, inverse governing-skill, and documented modifiers. Grapple
> initiation is another special move and therefore uses
> `SpecialMoveBaseStaminaCost = 4`. `GrappleStaminaCostPerRound = 2` applies
> only to ongoing per-round grapple maintenance; the existing
> controller/controlled role multiplier applies to that base before
> `costs.Calc`. The owner considers base 2 appropriate for ongoing grapple
> actions.

The plan discussion used the shorthand `SpecialMoveCostBase` and
`GrappleCostBase`; the table above records their exact implementation names so
Task 2 cannot accidentally charge grapple initiation at the maintenance base or
apply the maintenance role multiplier after `costs.Calc`.
