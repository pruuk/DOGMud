Tap and long press (first ideas)

LookTap: look at the room. Long press: a list of what is here to look at (people, creatures, items, things named in the room), visible ones only.
⚔ ratTap: attack the rat. Long press: the other moves against it (trip, bash, and so on), with ones on cooldown dimmed.
⟳ RallyTap: renew it now. Long press: renew it automatically every time it drops (builds a trigger you can see and remove in Automation).
Talk TokeTap: talk. Long press: consider, look at, or give to Toke.
⋯ exitsTap: a short list of the room's other exits (southeast, a portal, "in").
ScoreTap: your status sheet.
MoreTap: the rest of the contextual commands that did not fit (Consider rat, Talk Toke, a second buff), in a short list.

The squeeze: five command slots is tight for Talk, Look, Score, Consider, an attack, and one or more buffs at once. Options: fewer fixed (Look only), or a third deck row only during combat (about 2 rows of game text). This is the command bar's own design (#779); the layout just reserves the space.
