Moving by touch: deck and Map tab (touch arc spec 2, #781)

Approved by the owner 2026-11-22 for the spec (Malia was away; her look comes before plan 2b is built). Symbols: an arrow with its letter for compass and diagonal exits, an ISO 7001 style stairs pictogram for up and down. Dark room: option O, olive grey.

Galaxy S21, 1:1, full screen. Rooms, exits and the opening lines of each description are real game data (descriptions cut short). The command slots on the right of the deck are left empty: they belong to the command bar (spec 3). Kinds are told apart by shape, edge and marks, never by colour alone.
