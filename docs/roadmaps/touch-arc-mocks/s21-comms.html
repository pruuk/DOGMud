3. Reply in a box inside Comms. Reply opens a reply box at the bottom of the Comms tab, under the message you are answering, and the keyboard. The tab stays (typing inside a tab keeps it, today's rule); the game text shrinks to a line. Send, or Enter, sends it on the same channel; closing the keyboard drops the box. The command box and tab bar are under the keyboard. A little more to build, and you never lose sight of what you are answering.
